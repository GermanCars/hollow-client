# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

The first polished release.

- 158 modules for combat, movement, rendering, the world and chat, with a key, hold mode and settings for each.
- Printer and AutoBuild for Litematica schematics.
- One-window menu with search, themes, friends and profiles.
- 36 HUD elements and a HUD editor.
- Ice cavern start menu. The camera flies to a new chamber for each screen.
- Frosted glass for buttons, sliders, lists, tabs, tooltips, pop-ups, inventories, the hotbar and chat.
- Its own typeface in the whole game, with the game's letters filling in for other alphabets.
